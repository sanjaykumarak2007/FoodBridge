Hygiene & Quality Standards

  • Safe Serving Window

    Food should be prepared within the last 4-6 hours or stored chilled immediately.

  • Hygienic Packaging

    Food in stainless steel trays, sealed foil pans, or food-grade containers is preferred.

  • Zero Cost to Donor

    Our crew handles full pickup, thermal transport, and distribution free of charge.

  • Tax & Social Recognition

    Receive a FoodBridge Zero-Waste Partner digital certificate for your brand (Proposed Program).
