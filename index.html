Simplified Workflow

How FoodBridge Works

Our rapid 4-step pipeline ensures surplus food goes from banquet table to community plate safely, hygienically, and with zero hassle.

01

Donor Submits Surplus

Hotels or event hosts quickly submit available dishes, quantity in KG, preparation time, and photo via our easy donation portal.

02

Admin Review & Safety Check

FoodBridge coordinators evaluate the freshness window, hygiene guidelines, and approve the pickup request immediately.

03

Rapid Crew Dispatch

Our Food Response Crew collects the food in insulated thermal containers and transports it to nearby distribution centers.

04

Digital Board & Distribution

Approved food status broadcasts on outdoor community Digital Display Boards so citizens without smartphones know hot meals are ready!

Global Alignment

Supporting the UN Global Goals

FoodBridge directly accelerates India's commitment toward the 2030 United Nations Sustainable Development Goals.

SDG 2

Zero Hunger

End hunger, achieve food security and improved nutrition. FoodBridge ensures surplus nutrient-dense meals reach vulnerable urban clusters rather than garbage bins.

🎯 Target: Immediate community meal accessibility
SDG 11

Sustainable Cities & Communities

Make cities inclusive, safe, resilient, and sustainable. By installing public Digital Boards, we bridge the digital divide so homeless citizens can locate warm meals.

🏙️ Target: Non-smartphone accessible civic aid
SDG 12

Responsible Consumption & Production

Substantially reduce food waste at the retail and consumer levels, and reduce food losses along food supply chains including hotel and catering waste.

🌱 Target: Zero edible food to municipal landfills
